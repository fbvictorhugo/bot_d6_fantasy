# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the D6 Fantasy Discord bot (the "Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use it in Discord servers.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect and process the following information:

- Discord user ID
- Discord username and discriminator or display name
- Server ID and server name where the Bot is used
- Commands and interactions sent to the Bot
- Dice roll results generated through the Bot
- Basic usage metadata needed to operate the Bot (for example, command logs for troubleshooting and service reliability)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, or private messages.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

- Provide and maintain the Bot's functionality
- Process dice rolls and gameplay interactions
- Debug errors and improve reliability
- Enforce the Bot's intended usage and prevent abuse
- Respond to support requests or operational issues

## 3. Data Storage

The Bot may store limited operational data such as command usage logs and roll history for service reliability, debugging, or maintenance purposes.

We do not sell user data. We do not use personal data for targeted advertising.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the operation of the Bot, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, or compliance with legal obligations. If data is no longer needed, it may be deleted or anonymized.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Bot may use third-party services such as Discord, hosting infrastructure providers, or analytics tools required to operate and monitor the service. These services are subject to their own privacy practices.

## 6. User Rights

Users may request information about data stored related to their account or Discord activity by contacting the Bot owner or administrator responsible for the service. Depending on the applicable law and operational context, users may request deletion of data that is no longer necessary.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, or the minimum age defined by applicable local law. If we learn that a user under the required age is interacting with the Bot, we may restrict access or remove relevant data.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the relevant bot documentation or service channel. Continued use of the Bot after changes are made indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

## 10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Bot owner or server administrator responsible for the service.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
